# Goblin Tax: Rage Run — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 October 2026

Goblin Tax: Rage Run is a small browser game prototype. This release does not include advertising SDKs, analytics, user accounts, online leaderboards, or real-money purchases.

## Information processed

- **Local game progress:** The game stores coins, upgrade levels, the selected cosmetic, and high scores in your browser's local storage on your device. This data is used only to keep your local game progress. It is not sent to the developer by this build.
- **Sharing:** If you press Share Score, your device/browser may open its own share interface. The text you choose to share is handled by the apps or services you select.
- **Hosting logs:** If you access the game from a hosting provider, that provider may process standard technical request data (such as IP address and browser details) under its own privacy policy.

## Children

This prototype is not designed to collect personal information from children. It has no chat, social account, or user-generated content features.

## Retention and deletion

Local game data remains in your browser until you clear the site's storage or browser data. Clearing it will reset your progress.

## Future changes

This policy applies only to this prototype. If advertising, analytics, online services, or purchases are added, the game and this policy must be updated before those features launch, and any required consent and store disclosures must be implemented.
