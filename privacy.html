Goblin Tax: Rage Run — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how information may be processed when you visit and play Goblin Tax: Rage Run, a browser-based game project.

Game data stored on your device

The game may store progress such as coins, upgrade levels, selected cosmetics and high scores in your browser's local storage. This helps preserve your progress between visits. This locally stored game data is not intentionally sent to the developer by the game.

You can delete this data by clearing the site's storage or browser data. Doing so may reset your progress.

Advertising and cookies

This website uses Google AdSense code and may display advertisements if the site is approved and ads are enabled. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertisements, subject to your choices and applicable requirements.

These technologies and related processing may involve information such as your browser or device information, IP address, pages visited and interactions with advertisements. Google and its partners process information under their own applicable privacy policies.

For information about Google's data practices, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required, advertising technologies and personalised advertising will be subject to the relevant consent choices. You can manage available advertising settings through Google's Ad Settings.

Consent and privacy choices

Visitors may be shown a consent message to make choices about advertising cookies and the use of personal information. The website's advertising and consent settings must be configured appropriately before ads are served to visitors in regions where consent requirements apply.

Sharing scores

If you use a sharing feature, your browser or device may open a sharing interface. Information you choose to share is handled by the apps or services you select.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request data, to deliver, secure and maintain the service. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for more information.

Children

The game does not provide user accounts, chat or online leaderboards in this version. It is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

Data retention

Local game progress remains in your browser until you clear it. Technical logs and advertising-related information may be retained by the relevant service providers according to their policies and retention practices.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. To exercise rights relating to Google advertising, use Google's available privacy and advertising controls. For other questions about this game, contact the publisher using the project link below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change. The effective date above will be updated when appropriate.
